Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI redesign work?

We use a diffusion‑based model fine‑tuned on millions of marketing creatives. It analyses the source poster and suggested themes before generating alternatives.

Can I keep my brand colors?

Yes. Upload a brand‑guideline JSON and the engine will respect your palettes, fonts, and logo placement.

What file formats are supported?

Upload PNG, JPEG, PDF for the base poster. Export options include PNG, JPEG, PDF, and 4K WebP.

Is my data safe?

All uploads are encrypted at rest and processed in isolated containers. We comply with GDPR & CCPA.
